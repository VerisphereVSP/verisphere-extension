Verisphere is in your browser.

Check any claim you read, on any site.

1
Select a sentence

Any sentence on any page — a news article, a Reddit comment, a Quora answer, Wikipedia.

2
Right-click → Check on Verisphere

A side panel opens with the claims it states and where the stake stands on each. Nothing happens on a page until you ask.

3
Take a side — or be the first

Stake to support or challenge it there, share it with the standing and a link, or put VSP on the table for a friend. If nobody has staked on it yet, the panel offers to create the claim from your sentence.

Claims settle every day: the side the score favours gains VSP, the other side loses it. Right and early gains the most.

On English Wikipedia the extension also underlines the sentences that already have a claim; hover to see the score, click to open it.

Staking in the panel uses MetaMask on the page. If your wallet is a passkey, Back it in the app opens the same claim in Verisphere, where your passkey works — one tap.

Open How to Play for the whole game. The extension reads only from app.verisphere.co and never holds keys or funds; it does nothing on a page until you select text and ask.
